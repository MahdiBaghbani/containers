# Funding

DockyPody and this container image repository are built and maintained by
Mahdi Baghbani as part of ongoing work on Open Cloud Mesh (OCM). That work is
only possible because a few organizations chose to put real money into open
source infrastructure, and I am grateful to them.

## NLnet

NLnet backed this work early on. It is funded through the NGI0 Core Fund, a fund
established by NLnet with financial support from the European Commission's Next
Generation Internet programme, under the aegis of DG Communications Networks,
Content and Technology under grant agreement No 101092990.
